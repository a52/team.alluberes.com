## 🎯 The Opportunity

**Challenge**: Product team spending 20-30% of time on routine documentation work (non-strategic)
- Formatting descriptions consistently
- Filling missing requirements
- Standardizing labels & grammar
- Manual linking between items
- Maintaining audit trails

**Constraint**: To grow from 5 → 8-10 projects, would require hiring 2-3 more Product Owners
- Adds $395k-$593k/year in salary costs
- 60-100% team expansion for similar strategic output

**Solution**: AI-Assisted Product Owner agent that automates routine work
- Frees existing POs to focus on strategy
- Enables team scaling without proportional hiring
- Improves documentation quality & consistency
- Provides complete audit trail for compliance

## ⚠️ Risk Assessment

### Key Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|---|---|---|
| Agent adoption slower than expected | Medium | Medium | Phased rollout, training, monitoring |
| Over-reliance on automation | Low | Low | Agent is enhancement; manual fallback available |
| Technical complexity in rules | Medium | Low | Extensive testing, conservative rule set |
| Azure API rate limiting | Low | Medium | Early spike testing, exponential backoff |

**Overall Risk Level**: 🟢 **LOW-MEDIUM** (manageable with mitigations in place)
